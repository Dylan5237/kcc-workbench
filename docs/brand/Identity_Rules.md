# Identity Rules

## Brand essence
Arckeep preserves the conditions required to resume work: where work stopped, what was decided, why it was decided, and what should happen next. The value is continuity, not archival volume.

## Visual concept
The approved symbol compresses three ideas into one sharp mark:
- **A:** the dominant rising structure and the product name's initial.
- **K / handoff:** the internal angled cut reads as a relay-like handoff and continuation.
- **Intentional open cut:** the mark is not fully closed; the missing segment creates a visual place for the next continuation.

The mark should feel compact, decisive, memorable, and slightly seal-like, while remaining modern and suitable for a Windows application icon.

## Naming
- Primary brand name: **Arckeep**
- Chinese auxiliary name: **续境**
- Brand line: **留住当下，延续未来**

Do not routinely combine all three in one lockup.
