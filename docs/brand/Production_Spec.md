# Production Specification

## Master artwork
- Default logo: Symbol + Arckeep custom wordmark.
- The Chinese auxiliary name and the brand line are not default lockup elements.
- Master vector color: Deep Charcoal `#232323`.
- Reverse: Warm Ivory `#F5F2EA` on sufficiently dark backgrounds.

## Clear space
Maintain clear space equal to at least 25% of the symbol height around a standalone symbol, and at least the cap-height of the wordmark around lockups.

## Minimum size
- Symbol only: 16 px digital; 8 mm print.
- Horizontal lockup: 96 px digital; 24 mm print.
- Stacked lockup: 120 px digital; 28 mm print.

## Do not
- Stretch, skew, outline, rotate, re-space, or redraw the mark.
- Add shadows, glows, gradients, chrome, metallic foil, bevel, or 3D effects.
- Recolor the master mark arbitrarily.
- Attach `续境` or the brand line to every usage of the master logo.
